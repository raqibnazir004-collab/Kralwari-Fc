Built by the community, for the community

Founded in 2018 in Kralwari, Chadoora, Budgam, Kralawari FC is more than a football club — it's a home for local talent. Born from a shared passion for the game, KFC brings together young players determined to represent their community with pride, on and off the pitch.

Built on teamwork, discipline, and relentless hard work, the club gives local youth a real platform to compete, sharpen their skills, and rise together. From local leagues to local tournaments, every match is a step forward — for the players, and for Kralwari.
